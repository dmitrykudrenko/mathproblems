Probability · 3 doors

The Monty Hall problem

You're on a game show and pick one of three doors. The host, who knows where the car is, opens another door to reveal a goat and offers a switch. Should you take it?

Intuition50 / 50
Actually2/3 to switch

The most famous puzzle about conditional probability. In 1990 thousands of readers, including mathematicians with PhDs, insisted the correct answer was wrong.

Bayes' theorem · medical tests

The false positive paradox

1% of people have a disease. The test is 99% accurate, and you test positive. What is the chance you're really ill?

Intuition99%
Actually50%

A puzzle about the base rate fallacy: when a disease is rare, even an accurate test raises as many false alarms as true finds.

A match as long as a floorboard is wide drops onto a wooden floor at random. How often does it cross a seam between boards?

About

This is a collection of interactive explanations of famous math paradoxes and probability puzzles: the Monty Hall problem, the birthday paradox and more. Each page combines the puzzle, a game, a simulation, several independent explanations and harder variants.

Everything runs in the browser, with no sign-up, in English and Ukrainian. The site was built together with AI, and new puzzles are added over time.

In job interviews I like to discuss logic puzzles where intuition pulls you towards the wrong answer even though there is an exact mathematical solution. Whether the candidate gets it right has never mattered much to me. What matters is whether they can question a wrong assumption in conversation, and, when they are right, how well they can explain their idea or take apart someone else's.

One day I was explaining one of the best-known of these puzzles, the one from the film 21, to my son, and later to friends. I noticed that each person needed a different explanation, depending on how they think. That gave me the idea for this project: visualise these puzzles, offer several simple explanations, and push them further for the mathematically curious.

I built the first few puzzles together with AI, just to test the idea. But I liked the project so much that it was hard to stop, so there will be more puzzles here.
