The Monty Hall problem

You're on a game show and pick one of three doors. The host, who knows where the car is, opens another door to reveal a goat and offers a switch. Should you take it?

Intuition50 / 50
Actually2/3 to switch

The most famous puzzle about conditional probability. In 1990 thousands of readers, including mathematicians with PhDs, insisted the correct answer was wrong.
