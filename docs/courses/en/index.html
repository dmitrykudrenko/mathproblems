Paradoxes gathered into a path for your work

Each course tells one story for a particular kind of reader. It translates classic puzzles into the language of your work, adds short cases, sends you to the full puzzles in the catalogue and to terms in the glossary, and ends with a checklist.

Course · for managers

How not to fool yourself with data

Average order +24%, 92% satisfied, app users spend ×2.3. Three parts from simple to hard: how to read the numbers in a report, who got into the data and who is missing, and what follows from what. Surveys, cohorts, rankings, attribution, fraud filters and an exam of six reports.

13 chapters · 9 catalogue puzzles Course · for marketers

A/B testing paradoxes

The dashboard says "B +17%, confidence 94%". Three parts from simple to hard: how to read a dashboard, how to plan and report a test, and what only shows over a year. Subject lines, AI variants, a Bayesian "chance to beat" and an exam of six dashboards.

20 chapters · 13 catalogue puzzles Course · for product teams

Does the feature work? Impact without an experiment

The report says: the feature's users retain twice as well. Three parts from simple to hard: why feature numbers mislead, how to estimate impact without an A/B test, what cohorts and totals hide. Staggered rollouts, difference in differences, thresholds, holdouts and an exam of six reports.

15 chapters · 5 catalogue puzzles Course · for candidates

How to pass interviews with puzzles

Two ropes each burn for an hour, but unevenly. How do you measure 45 minutes? Three parts: reasoning out loud, tools for logic puzzles, probability and what to do when you're stuck. It ends with a mock interview with a timer and hints.
